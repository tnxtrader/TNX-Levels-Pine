DISC ENGINE v3 — BUILD FRAMEWORK

Ten DISC families, each searched only over its lookback Q (3 to 500, 23 geometric steps), its band level (80, 90, 95) and its side. That is 1,380 cells per market, five markets, and no entry conditions to tune. Every family carries one fixed exit.

The program runs in five stages. Stage I proves an edge before any cost: it must beat the drift of the same holding time at the same time of day, survive the family test and the Q-band test, and beat its own luck in every randomization test (999 draws each, with the whole selection repeated inside). Only then does Stage II apply TopstepX costs for the micro and the mini contract. Stage III fixes Q by walk-forward and proves the net result. Stage IV opens Validation and the sealed Test once each. Stage V runs the Pine script live under a kill switch. A stage without a survivor ends the program, and that is a valid result.

The engine itself is defined in B.4, the families in DISC_FAMILIES.html, the reasons for every choice in DISC_RESEARCH.md, and the chart implementation in DISC_ENGINE_v3.pine.

THE LOCKED PILLARS

1LUCK IS THE DEFAULT HYPOTHESIS

A result counts only after it beats its own luck in every randomization test (B.11), with the search over families, Q and levels repeated inside each randomization.

2EDGE BEFORE COSTS

Stage I uses gross results only. No cell sees its costs before it holds an Edge Certificate.

3NO LEAKAGE

Every engine value reads bars before or at t. The band at bar t is fixed before bar t prints. The universe, the engine and every threshold are hashed before any result exists. Validation and Test are opened once each.

4NOTHING TUNED BUT Q AND THE LEVEL

Every constant of the engine and the families is fixed in advance from a published value or a random-walk simulation, never from data. The only search is over Q, the level and the side, and every searched cell is counted.

5CONSERVATIVE EXECUTION, TOPSTEP RULES

Decide at the close, fill at the next open. The stop wins a tie and a gap fills at the open. Stop fills are stressed (B.14). Every position is flat by 15:08 CT, and none is held across a session or a roll.

6BOTH CONTRACTS, BOTH SIDES

Every certified edge is costed for micro and mini. Every family runs long and short, as exact mirrors.

A.2 — The program in plain words

  1. F0–F2. Clean the data and lock away the last 40% of history. Prove the engine is exact. Prove the tests rarely find an edge on fake markets without one, and do find a planted one. Freeze the 1,380 cells per market.
  2. P1. For every cell, measure what its trades earned before costs, against simply holding for the same time at the same hour.
  3. P2. Ask each family on each market and side as a whole whether it beats chance.
  4. P3. Inside the families that pass, keep only cells that work across several neighbouring Q values, not at one lucky Q.
  5. P4. Try to destroy each survivor with 999 shuffled markets (the whole search repeated on each), random entry times, shifted signals, worse stop fills, other years and regimes, and many out-of-sample paths. Then deflate by every trial.
  6. P5. Apply the micro and mini costs, and keep only edges that pay them at least twice over.
  7. P6–P7. Choose Q inside its band using only older years. Prove the net result again, and check that the risk fits the Topstep account.
  8. P8–P9. Run on the locked Validation years once, then the locked Test years once.
  9. P10. Load the card into the Pine script, check it matches, run 30 simulated trades, and stop it the moment it breaks.

A.3 — The gate law

  1. Every gate has a fixed threshold written in the configuration before F2.
  2. Unit gates are columns g_*, and a unit continues only if all its hard gates pass. Report figures decide nothing.
  3. A phase is PASS with at least one survivor, else STOP. The next phase refuses to start after a STOP.
  4. Selection and testing never reuse one statistic on the same data. P1 measures, P2 selects families, P3 selects cells, and P4 attacks them with tests P2–P3 did not use.

PART B — STANDING LAWS

B.1 — Never changes

  1. Raw files are never modified.
  2. The engine, the families, the universe, the configuration and every threshold are frozen at F2. Any change starts a new program, and the old trials stay in the ledger.
  3. No gate is loosened after a result, and nothing is added because of a result.
  4. Validation and Test never change a choice.
  5. Nothing is ranked on win rate, profit factor or in-sample equity.

B.2 — The data law

  1. One 30-minute file per market: timestamp, open, high, low, close, volume, contract, in CT, continuous and back-adjusted by addition.
  2. Every rule and result uses price differences in ATR or ticks. Ratios of price are never used, because back-adjustment by addition destroys them.
  3. A roll is a change of the contract column. No position is held across it. Volume is the continuous series' volume; the slot medians (B.4 E6) compare it only with itself at the same time of day.
  4. Timestamps are bar open times in CT local time (America/Chicago, with daylight saving), so every clock rule follows the exchange in summer and in winter.
  5. Holidays and early closes. A session has the bars it has and ends at its last bar on the exchange calendar. A missing anchor bar (OB, SB, IB or the European-open bar) leaves that anchor empty for the session, and a rule that reads an empty anchor does not fire. Slot histories only take the slots a session has.

B.3 — The execution law

  1. Decide at the close of bar t. Fill at the open of bar t + 1 of the same session.
  2. Sessions: the CME day starts at 17:00 CT, and a gap of 120 minutes or more also starts one.
  3. Flat by 15:08 CT. The last tradable bar is known at its own close: it is the bar whose next scheduled bar would close after 15:08, or the session's last bar on the exchange calendar. There is no entry from it, and open trades close at its close. If that bar is missing from the data, the first bar after it closes the trade at its close, as TradingView does.
  4. One position at a time.
  5. Exits are checked on every bar from the entry bar, in this order:
    1. the open through the stop: exit at the open;
    2. the low (the high for shorts) through the stop: exit at the stop;
    3. the last tradable bar: exit at its close;
    4. the family's close-based exit: exit at the next open;
    5. the time cap: exit at the next open.
    After a stop exit, a new signal on the same bar may enter. After a close-based or time exit, the next eligible signal bar is the exit-fill bar.
  6. Shorts run on mirrored prices (o, h, l, c) → (−o, −l, −h, −c).
Basis: [R10]; Topstep flattening [R26]; TradingView order processing [R75].

B.4 — The DISC engine (v3)

Every element is computed on the side's mirrored prices, for one (Q, level). Every value at bar t uses bars before or at t. Constants are fixed and never tuned.

E1  the bar
    R = max(H − L, 1e−10)      MID = (H + L)/2
    CLV  = (2C − H − L)/R      body = |C − O|/R      (both 0 when H = L)
    dir  = +1 if C > O, −1 if C < O, 0 otherwise

E2  DISC (a bounded fair price inside the bar; tanh = the inverse Fisher transform)
    zs   = 1.05·CLV + 0.62·body·CLV − 0.18·dir·body − 0.04·dir          (|zs| ≤ 1.89)
    DISC = MID + 0.5·R·tanh(zs)          tanh(x) = (e^{2x} − 1)/(e^{2x} + 1)

E3  the prior window (the current bar is NOT in its own window: no √(Q−1) ceiling)
    μ_t = mean(DISC_{t−Q} … DISC_{t−1})
    σ_t = sqrt(mean((DISC_i − μ_t)²)), i = t−Q … t−1        (two-pass)
    ready_t = t ≥ Q and σ_t > 0          z_t = (DISC_t − μ_t)/σ_t

E4  the K census and the bands (fixed before bar t prints)
    tail = (100 − L)/200            N_K = max(⌈100/tail⌉, 20·Q)
    𝒵_t  = the last N_K values of z_j with j < t and ready_j (kept sorted)
    K_U  = Q7(𝒵_t, 1 − tail)        K_L = −Q7(𝒵_t, tail)
    Q7   = linear interpolation between order statistics: h = (n − 1)p,
           Q7 = s[⌊h⌋] + (h − ⌊h⌋)(s[⌊h⌋+1] − s[⌊h⌋])       (numpy "linear", Hyndman–Fan type 7)
    bands ready: |𝒵_t| = N_K, K_U > 0, K_L > 0, ready_t
    Upper_t = μ_t + K_U·σ_t          Lower_t = μ_t − K_L·σ_t

E5  ATR(14), Wilder: TR_0 undefined; TR_t = max(H−L, |H−C_{t−1}|, |L−C_{t−1}|);
    ATR_14 = mean(TR_1 … TR_14); ATR_t = (13·ATR_{t−1} + TR_t)/14

E6  slot-relative range and volume (slot = the bar's open time)
    rr_t = R_t / median(R of the same slot, previous 20 sessions)     (needs 20)
    rv_t = V_t / median(V of the same slot, previous 20 sessions)
    shock_t = rr_t ≥ 2 and rv_t ≥ 2

E7  trend strength, against a random walk
    TS_q = |C_t − C_{t−q}| / Σ_{i=t−q+1..t}|C_i − C_{i−1}| · √q        (needs t ≥ q)
    random walk: median 0.85, 90th percentile 2.04 for every q ≥ 10
    choppy: TS_Q ≤ 0.85          up-trend at scale q: TS_q ≥ 2.0 and C_t − C_{t−q} > 0

E8  session clock: OB = the cash-open bar; SB = the bar ending at the settlement; IB = SB − 1 bar;
    EUB = the European-open bar (01:00 CT by default); O_day = open of OB; S_prev = close of the previous session's SB
    rms_slot = RMS of (DISC − O_day) at the same slot over the previous Q sessions (OPEN_BREAK)
    rms_IB   = RMS of (DISC − S_prev) at IB over the previous Q sessions (CLOSE_DRIFT)

E9  the excursion machine (Lower band; Upper is the mirror), W(Q) = max(5, ⌈√Q⌉)
    bands not ready → ARMED
    ARMED:   C < Lower → OUT (excursion low = L, shock latch = shock)
    OUT:     excursion low = min(·, L); latch |= shock;
             C > Lower → RECOVERY event (with the excursion low and the latch) → ARMED
             else if t − t_out ≥ W(Q) → EXPIRED (never traded)
    EXPIRED: C > Lower → ARMED (silently)
Basis and measured reasons (DISC_RESEARCH.md): the 11-term score of v2 collapses exactly to these symmetric terms (max change 1.17% of the bar range, mirror error 0); the in-window z of v2 is capped at √(Q−1) (Samuelson) and saturated every Q ≤ 6; the v2 burst label was CLIMAX on 98.6% of cash-open bars, while the slot-relative shock is about 32% at every hour; the K census equals numpy's quantile exactly; the beyond-band share ÷ tail is 0.94–1.07 at every Q from 3 to 100 [R50–R54].

B.5 — The family law

  1. The ten families, their exact entries and their fixed exits are in DISC_FAMILIES.html:
    • Reversion: BAND_RECOVERY, QUIET_RECOVERY, SHOCK_RECOVERY.
    • Trend: TREND_DIP, BAND_RIDE, STOP_CASCADE, SHOCK_IMPULSE.
    • Session: OPEN_BREAK, CLOSE_DRIFT, EURO_OPEN_REVERSAL.
  2. A family's exit is part of the family. Exits are not searched.
  3. Every family runs on the same engine build of its (market, Q, level, side). No family has its own K.
  4. Families may share bars. Each family is judged on its own trades. The P3 dedupe (Jaccard > 0.30 within a market) removes duplicate bets before P4.

B.7 — The account law

The account size, its maximum loss limit, the daily loss limit, the micro counting rule, the permitted contracts, the maximum loss per trade and the drawdown budget are read from the trader's Topstep dashboard and written into the configuration before F2. Published Combine parameters: 50K → $2,000 MLL, 5 minis; 100K → $3,000, 10 minis; 150K → $4,500, 15 minis [R48]. One contract per strategy.

B.8 — The leakage law

  1. Causality is tested (F1): changing every bar after t changes no element and no signal at or before t.
  2. The bands at t use z values before t only. The slot medians and slot RMS use previous sessions only.
  3. Test rows are hashed in F0 and not loaded before P9. Validation is untouched before P8.
  4. The engine, the families and every threshold are hashed in F2.
  5. Earlier DISC campaigns on these Dev data enter the ledger with their Keff, and any v2 family they studied carries the Dev-informed flag ★: stricter exams at P8.

B.9 — The overfitting law and the Q-band rule

  1. No constant is fitted. The only searched parameters are Q (23 grid values), the level (3) and the side (2).
  2. Q-band rule. A cell is selectable only inside a Q band: at least 3 consecutive grid Q values at the same level and side whose cells all have excess > 0 and Newey-West t ≥ 2, at least one of them a P3 discovery. The cell carried forward is the middle Q of the longest band. A scale that exists at one Q only is noise.
  3. Every selection is followed by a test on data the selection did not use (CPCV, walk-forward, Validation, Test).
  4. Every search is measured with PBO over the family's 69 cells, and every deciding Sharpe ratio is deflated by the ledger N.

B.12 — Stability

Buckets: Dev block, year, volatility regime (R_prev ÷ its 250-session median, terciles), session bucket (ASIA 17:00–02:00, EUROPE 02:00–08:30, US 08:30–15:08) and weekday. Every bucket with at least 30 trades is judged, and none may be significantly negative (its 97.5% bootstrap upper bound below 0).

B.13 — Sample floors

A cell needs ≥ 100 Dev trades in P1. A bucket is judged at ≥ 30 trades. P6 out-of-sample needs ≥ 50. P8 and P9 need ≥ 30 per strategy. Incubation needs ≥ 30.

B.15 — Calibration and power

  1. False certificates. Stage I runs on 100 N1 null markets per instrument. At most 5 of 100 may produce a certificate.
  2. Power. Stage I runs on null markets with a planted edge: closes beyond a 40-bar 2-σ band are pulled back at sizes of 0.05, 0.10 and 0.20 ATR per bar, 20 runs each. The minimum detectable edge is the smallest size certified in at least 80% of runs, and it is compared with each contract's cost in P5.
  3. Inside these runs the randomization tests use B = 199.

B.16 — The verification law

  1. A slow, bar-by-bar reference written from B.3, B.4 and the family page must equal the fast engine on synthetic markets, on every element, signal and trade, both sides. A line-by-line transliteration of DISC_ENGINE_v3.pine with TradingView's order processing must equal it too.
  2. Mirror: the short side on a market equals the long side on the mirrored market.
  3. Causality: future bars never change past elements or signals.
  4. The K census equals numpy's linear quantile. The band calibration (beyond-band share ÷ tail) is within 0.8–1.25 at Q 3, 5, 20 and 100.
  5. Statistics equal independent references (statsmodels HAC and BH; Monte Carlo for the DSR null; known inputs for PBO, CPCV, the bootstrap and Simes).
  6. On pure noise the families show no edge, and a planted edge is found (B.15).
  7. Second engine: an independent implementation on another framework (Backtrader) reproduces every trade of the reference, both sides.
  8. Property tests (Hypothesis, thousands of random bar sets): DISC stays inside [low, high], mirrors exactly and equals MID on a flat bar; the K quantile equals numpy's.
  9. Holiday markets: items 1, 3 and 7 also hold on a synthetic market with early closes, missing anchor bars, a halt inside a session and missing sessions.
Basis: property-based testing [R74].

B.17 — Closeout, amendment and seal

Each phase writes a report (what it did, tried, changed and found, and its gates) with tables, plots and a PASS or STOP file. The report is read before the next phase. A change after F2 opens a new program. P9 writes a lock with the hash of what it opened Test for; a different set later is refused or marked contaminated.

FOUNDATION

F0 — DATA & CLOCK

Job

Certify the five files, build sessions, rolls, the session clock and the windows, and seal Test.

Steps

  1. Load and sort each file. Remove duplicate timestamps and enforce OHLC containment, counting both.
  2. Check the 30-minute grid, the tick grid, and that volume is non-negative and non-zero on most bars.
  3. Build sessions, rolls, the flat-by mask and the clock (OB, IB, SB, EUB) per market from the exchange schedule. List every gap of 120 minutes or more inside a trading week and match it to the CME holiday calendar.
  4. Cut the windows (A.4), and split Dev into 6 blocks, 10 groups and 16 blocks.
  5. Hash the Test rows.
Gates (hard):
  • repairs ≤ 0.1% of bars;
  • ≥ 99% of timestamps and prices on their grids;
  • ≥ 250 sessions per window;
  • OB and SB present in ≥ 95% of Dev sessions;
  • every gap of 120 minutes or more inside a week is a calendar holiday or halt;
  • volume > 0 on ≥ 99% of bars.
Outputs: data QA, repairs, splits, costs (micro and mini), clock coverage. Plots: split timeline, bars per slot, volume by slot.

F1 — ENGINE ACCEPTANCE & CALIBRATION

Job

Prove the engine, the families and the statistics do exactly what B.3, B.4, A.5 and the family page say, and measure false certificates and power.

Plain words: test the tester. No edge on fake markets without one, and a found edge on fake markets with one planted.

Steps

  1. Hand cases for every exit rule of B.3: stop, gap, tie, flat-by, close exit, time cap, one position.
  2. The verification law B.16, items 1–5 and 7–9.
  3. Null uniformity: the p-values of N1–N5 on 200 null markets (Kolmogorov–Smirnov).
  4. Calibration and power (B.15).
Gates (hard):
  • every hand case and B.16 test passes;
  • no null rejects uniformity at 1%;
  • ≤ 5 of 100 null markets certify per instrument.
Report: power curves and the MDE.
Outputs: acceptance table, calibration table, power curves, MDE table.

F2 — PRE-REGISTRATION & LEDGER

Job

Freeze everything before any result exists.

Steps

  1. Write the 1,380 cells per market (family, Q, level, side, ★ flag).
  2. Hash the engine code, the family code, the configuration, the cell list and this framework together.
  3. Open the ledger with the earlier DISC campaigns' Keff.
  4. Run a signal census on Dev (counts only, no returns): signals per cell, and Jaccard overlap within each family.
Gate (hard): the hash is stored before P1 runs.
Outputs: cell list, hash, ledger, signal census.
STAGE I — EDGE BEFORE COSTS (gross, Dev only)

P1 — EVENT STUDY

Job

Measure every cell's gross trades with its family's own exit, against the matched drift. Nothing is selected.

Steps

  1. Run every cell on Dev: signals, trades with the fixed exit, gross R in ATR.
  2. Per trade, compute the excess over the matched drift (A.5).
  3. Per cell, compute: trades, mean gross, mean excess, the per-session series, Newey-West t and p, excess per Dev block, MFE/MAE, and the exit mix (stop / close / time / flat-by).
  4. Per market and family, compute Keff, PBO and Hansen SPA (report).
Gate (hard): ≥ 100 Dev trades per cell.
Outputs: cell table. Plots: t against Q for every family, level and side (the Q map); the t distribution against N(0,1).

P2 — FAMILY TEST

Job

Ask whether a family has any edge on a market and side, across its 69 cells, before judging single cells.

Steps

  1. Compute the family p (Simes and pooled) for each of the 100 family tests.
  2. Apply BH at q = 0.05 across the 100; R are selected.
Gate (hard): R ≥ 1.
Outputs: family table; family × market × side map.

P3 — CELL SELECTION (Q BANDS)

Job

Inside the selected families, keep only cells that live inside a Q band.

Steps

  1. Apply Benjamini–Bogomolov BH at 0.10·R/100 over each selected family's cells.
  2. Find the Q bands (B.9). Each band contributes its middle cell.
  3. Also require: excess > 0 in ≥ 4 of 6 Dev blocks.
  4. Dedupe within each market: drop a cell whose entries overlap a kept cell by Jaccard > 0.30.
Gates (hard, per cell): g_bb, g_qband, g_blocks. Phase: ≥ 1 cell.
Outputs: cell table with Q-band length and position; dedupe log; Q-map plots with the bands marked.

P4 — LUCK ELIMINATION & EDGE CERTIFICATE

Job

Try to destroy every surviving cell with tests P2–P3 did not use.

Plain words: make luck compete 999 times, with luck also allowed to pick its best Q and level. Then make the fills worse, shake the prices, split the years, and deflate by every trial.

Steps

  1. N1-S (999): rerun the whole family search, Q bands included, on each shuffled market.
  2. N2 and N3 (999 each); N4 (2,000); N5.
  3. Stop-fill stress, delay, price noise, Q and level neighbours, concentration, stability.
  4. CPCV paths of the family's selection; PBO over its 69 cells.
  5. Gross DSR at the ledger N; MinTRL against the Validation sessions.
Gates (hard, all required) — the Edge Certificate
  1. g_mcpt_sel N1-S p ≤ 0.01 · g_random N2 p ≤ 0.01 · g_shift N3 p ≤ 0.01
  2. g_ci 95% bootstrap lower bound of the excess > 0 · g_detrend detrended excess t ≥ 2
  3. g_stopfill excess > 0 with every stop fill 2 ticks worse
  4. g_noise t ≥ 2 in ≥ 90 of 100 price-noise copies · g_qnb both Q neighbours with excess > 0
  5. g_blocks5 ≥ 5 of 6 blocks positive · g_years ≥ 70% of years positive and positive without the best year · g_stable no judged bucket significantly negative · g_trim positive without the top 5%
  6. g_cpcv ≥ 7 of 9 paths positive · g_pbo PBO ≤ 0.20 · g_dsr gross DSR ≥ 0.95 · g_power MinTRL ≤ Validation sessions
Phase: ≥ 1 certificate.
Outputs: certificate table. Plots: null histograms with the real value, CPCV paths, Q map with neighbours, stop-fill stress, stability bars.
Closeout: Only certified cells meet costs. A STOP means DISC has no timing edge on these markets at this bar size that survives luck.
STAGE II — COSTS (micro and mini)

P5 — COST HURDLE

Job

Price every certified cell with both contracts.

Steps

  1. For each cell × contract (permitted contracts only): cost per trade in ATR (B.6), net per trade, net per-session series, Newey-West t.
  2. BCM; stresses (× 1.5, × 2, +1 tick per side); cost against the MDE.
Gates (hard, per cell × contract): net mean > 0 · net t ≥ 2 · BCM ≥ 2 · net > 0 at cost × 1.5 · net > 0 at +1 tick per side. Phase: ≥ 1 pair.
Outputs: cost table; gross excess against cost per trade, micro and mini.
STAGE III — CONSTRUCTION

P6 — WALK-FORWARD Q CHOICE

Job

Choose Q inside the cell's band without scoring a choice on the data it was chosen on. Exits stay as the family defines them.

Steps

  1. Folds k = 1…5 train on blocks B0…B(k−1). Choose the band Q with the highest 25th percentile of block means, after smoothing over its grid neighbours. Apply it unchanged to Bk.
  2. Stitch B1…B5 into the walk-forward out-of-sample (WF-OOS) net series.
  3. Final Q: the same rule on B0…B5.
  4. Ledger: Keff of the choices.
Gates (hard): WF-OOS trades ≥ 50; WF-OOS net mean > 0.

P7 — NET EVIDENCE & ACCOUNT FIT

Job

Prove the net result survives the Q choice, stress and time, and fits the account.

Steps

  1. WF-OOS net: bootstrap CI (2,000), PSR, net DSR at the ledger N, MinTRL.
  2. N2 and N3 on net R (999 each).
  3. Stop-fill stress, cost × 1.5, +1 tick, delay, Q neighbours, concentration, stability.
  4. Account fit for one contract: stop risk in dollars and the worst WF-OOS drawdown. Report a Combine simulation (bootstrap paths of daily net dollars under the trailing MLL).
Gates (hard):
  • CI lower bound > 0;
  • net DSR ≥ 0.95;
  • N2 and N3 p ≤ 0.05;
  • all stresses > 0;
  • MinTRL ≤ Validation sessions;
  • stop risk ≤ the configured maximum loss per trade;
  • worst drawdown ≤ the configured budget.
STAGE IV — EXAMS (one look each)

P8 — VALIDATION

Steps

  1. Run each P7 strategy once on Validation, frozen. The engine histories are warmed on the bars before Validation.
  2. Compute gross excess (matched drift built within Validation), net, stationary bootstrap (10,000), BH across strategies, N2 (999), cost × 1.5, and MinTRL against the Test sessions.
  3. Refutation test: build the comparison the family names in DISC_FAMILIES.html ("Refuted if") with the strategy's own Q, level, side and exit, and run it once on Validation.
Gates (hard):
  • trades ≥ 30;
  • gross excess > 0;
  • net > 0;
  • BH q ≤ 0.05 and N2 p ≤ 0.05 (both ≤ 0.01 for ★);
  • net > 0 at cost × 1.5;
  • MinTRL ≤ Test sessions;
  • not refuted: the mean gross R per trade is above the comparison's.

P9 — SEALED TEST

Steps

  1. Check and write the lock.
  2. Run once on Test: net per session, 95% CI (10,000), DSR with N = strategies tested, cost × 1.5, gross excess.
  3. Write a deployment card per survivor: market, contract, family, Q, level, side, costs, flat-by, Test CI, worst drawdown, ★.
Gates (hard):
  • trades ≥ 30;
  • CI lower bound > 0;
  • DSR ≥ 0.95;
  • net > 0 at cost × 1.5;
  • gross excess > 0;
  • not contaminated.
STAGE V — LIVE

P10 — PINE PARITY, INCUBATION & KILL SWITCH

Steps

  1. Load the card into DISC_ENGINE_v3.pine: family, Q, level, side and clock inputs, on the continuous contract, 30-minute bars, full session. Run the Pine Profiler on the full chart: the script must finish inside TradingView's execution-time limit (20 s on a basic plan, 40 s on the others).
  2. Parity replay on the Test dates, holiday sessions included, with commission 0 and slippage 0: every entry and exit bar and price must equal the research engine's. Then set the card's commission and slippage.
  3. Incubate in simulation for ≥ 30 trades.
  4. Repaint check: every week of incubation, replay the same bars in TradingView's bar replay. Every live signal and fill must be identical: nothing appears, moves or disappears after its bar has closed.
  5. Kill switch after every trade from the 30th: stop when the live mean R falls below the Test CI lower bound or the drawdown exceeds the worst historical drawdown.
  6. A killed strategy is never re-optimised.
Gates (hard): inside the execution-time limit; exact parity; no repaint; incubation inside the Test CI and the historical drawdown.

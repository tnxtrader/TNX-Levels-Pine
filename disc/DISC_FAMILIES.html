DISC FAMILIES v3

Ten entry families on the DISC v3 engine, in three groups. BAND_RECOVERY is kept and fixed. The other nine are rebuilt or new, each on a published market effect. Every family has one fixed exit and no tuned constant. Each one is searched only over Q (3 to 500), the band level (80, 90, 95) and the side. The engine is defined in DISC_FRAMEWORK.html, Part B.

Rules every family follows

  • Long form. Every rule below is the long rule. The short rule is the same rule on mirrored prices (open, high, low, close) → (−open, −low, −high, −close): Upper becomes Lower, a high becomes a low, a sign flips. The engine is exactly mirror-symmetric, so nothing else changes.
  • Timing. A family is decided at the close of its signal bar and filled at the open of the next bar of the same session. There is no entry from a session's last tradable bar (flat by 15:08 CT). One position at a time.
  • Exits, checked on every bar from the entry bar, in this order:
    1. the open through the stop: exit at the open;
    2. the low through the stop: exit at the stop;
    3. the session's last tradable bar: exit at its close;
    4. the family's close-based exit: exit at the next open;
    5. the time cap reached: exit at the next open.
    Every stop sits 1 tick beyond its level.
  • Axes.
    • Q on the grid 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 15, 19, 24, 31, 39, 49, 62, 78, 98, 124, 156, 197, 249, 314, 396, 500.
    • Q counts bars for families 1–7 and 10, and sessions for OPEN_BREAK and CLOSE_DRIFT.
    • Level 80, 90 or 95 is the band level for families 1–7 and 10. For 8 and 9 it maps to the normal quantile 1.2816, 1.6449 or 1.9600.
    • Both sides.
  • Gate. A family that reads a band fires only where the band is ready (census full, K_U and K_L > 0). A family that reads a slot history fires only once that history is full.
  • Constants. The only constants are TS 0.85 / 2.0 (random-walk quantiles), SHOCK ×2 (twice the slot median), SLOT_N 20 sessions, the shock close CLV ≥ 0.5, and the published horizons of families 7 and 10 (2 and 4 bars). None is ever tuned.

TS|C − C[q]| / Σ|ΔC| · √q; a random walk has median 0.85 and 90th percentile 2.0

Reversion

Market makers who absorb a burst of one-sided orders are paid when the price comes back. The trade waits for the return inside the band: the bounce literature shows the first break is not yet the reversal.

1

BAND_RECOVERY

Reversionkept from v2, fixed
Idea
A close beyond the band that returns inside quickly was a temporary imbalance, and price reverts toward the mean. This is the yardstick for families 2–4.
Entry
The recovery bar of a Lower-band excursion: a close below Lower, then a close above Lower within W(Q) bars.
Exit
The first close ≥ μ, at the next open. Stop 1 tick below the excursion low. Time cap 2·W(Q) bars. Flat-by.
Fixed vs v2
The band uses the prior-window z, so there is no Samuelson ceiling and Q 3–500 all work. The census is exact. The DISC is symmetric. The exit is defined.
Basis
Campbell, Grossman & Wang 1993; Nagel 2012; Heston, Korajczyk & Sadka 2010; contrarian Bollinger results (Lento et al. 2007)
2

QUIET_RECOVERY

Reversionnew
Idea
Reversion pays where the market itself is mean-reverting. A market choppier than a random walk has a low efficiency ratio for its horizon.
Entry
The BAND_RECOVERY event with TS_Q ≤ 0.85 on the recovery bar.
Exit
As BAND_RECOVERY.
Basis
Lo & MacKinlay 1988 (variance ratio < 1 means mean reversion); Kaufman efficiency ratio; Bianco & Renò 2006 (reversal outside high unexpected-volatility regimes). The threshold is the random-walk median, simulated.
3

SHOCK_RECOVERY

Reversionrebuilt from climax_exhaustion
Idea
A price move made on unusual volume AND an unusual range for that time of day is liquidity demand. When it comes back inside the band, the reversal pays the most.
Entry
The BAND_RECOVERY event whose excursion held at least one shock bar (from the first bar beyond the band to the recovery bar, inclusive).
Exit
As BAND_RECOVERY.
Fixed vs v2
The CLIMAX label compared a bar with the previous 4 hours and fired on 98.6% of cash-open bars. The shock is now measured against the same slot of previous sessions, so it fires on about a third at every hour.
Basis
Campbell, Grossman & Wang 1993 (high-volume moves reverse); Llorente et al. 2002 (reversal where trading is hedging, as in index futures); Nagel 2012; Andersen & Bollerslev 1997 (slot normalization)

Trend

Futures trend at weekly-to-monthly horizons. A strong trend at the family's own scale is required before a continuation is taken, and that strength is measured against a random walk.

4

TREND_DIP

Trendrebuilt from mean_pullback
Idea
A short-term overreaction against a longer trend reverses back into the trend: buy the dip of an up-trend once it has recovered.
Entry
The BAND_RECOVERY event with TS_4Q ≥ 2.0 and C − C[4Q] > 0 on the recovery bar.
Exit
The first close ≥ Upper, at the next open (the trend resumed). Stop 1 tick below the excursion low. Time cap 4·W(Q) bars. Flat-by.
Basis
Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012; Hurst, Ooi & Pedersen 2017; Kwon et al. 2020 (weekly commodity momentum); short-term liquidity reversal (above)
5

BAND_RIDE

Trendrebuilt
Idea
Closes that stay beyond the band while the market trends far more than a random walk are a trend being accepted.
Entry
The bar on which the run of consecutive closes above Upper reaches 2, with TS_Q ≥ 2.0 and C − C[Q] > 0. Needs Q ≥ 5 (TS ≤ √Q).
Exit
The first close < μ, at the next open. Stop 1 tick below the signal bar's μ. Flat-by.
Fixed vs v2
band_move and mean_dir (the 20th-percentile direction rules, about 80% UP/DOWN by construction) are replaced by one trend test with a random-walk null.
Basis
Moskowitz, Ooi & Pedersen 2012; Lo & MacKinlay 1988 (variance ratio > 1); Kaufman
6

STOP_CASCADE

Trendrebuilt from failed_reclaim
Idea
Stops cluster just beyond the extreme of a recent excursion. When a failed upper excursion's high is broken soon after, the triggered stops push price further.
Entry
After an Upper-band recovery (a close back below Upper) at bar r with excursion high X: the first close > X within W(Q) bars after r.
Exit
Stop 1 tick below the signal bar's low. Time cap W(Q) bars. Flat-by.
Basis
Osler 2005 (trends accelerate at stop-loss clusters; take-profit clusters reverse)
7

SHOCK_IMPULSE

Trendrebuilt from vol_burst_break
Idea
Unexpected volatility makes short-horizon index-futures returns positively autocorrelated. A shock bar that closes beyond the band near its high carries on briefly.
Entry
A shock bar with C > Upper and CLV ≥ 0.5.
Exit
Stop 1 tick below the shock bar's middle. Time cap 2 bars. Flat-by.
Basis
Bianco & Renò 2006, 2009 (unexpected volatility drives positive intraday serial correlation). Short hold, because the effect is short-lived.

Session

Effects tied to the trading clock, measured with the engine's DISC and Q.

8

OPEN_BREAK

Sessionnew
Idea
A move away from the open that is large for that time of day marks an imbalance that carries through the day. This is the noise-area breakout on DISC.
Entry
On day-session bars (OB ≤ bar < SB): d = (DISC − O_day) / rms_slot, where rms_slot is the root mean square of DISC − O_day at the same slot over the previous Q sessions. Enter on the first bar of the session with d ≥ z_L.
Exit
Stop 1 tick below O_day. Flat-by.
Basis
Zarattini, Aziz & Barbon 2024 (noise area, 2007–2024); Holmberg et al. 2013; Lundström 2017 (volatility states). Q here counts sessions; ZAB's 14 days is on the grid.
9

CLOSE_DRIFT

Sessionnew
Idea
The day's move since the previous settlement predicts the last half-hour (hedging demand). Thresholds survive out of sample better than signs.
Entry
At IB: s = (DISC − S_prev) / rms_IB, where rms_IB is the root mean square of DISC − S_prev at IB over the previous Q sessions. Enter if s ≥ z_L.
Exit
One bar: in at the open of SB, out at the next open, or at SB's close when SB is the last tradable bar.
Basis
Gao et al. 2018; Baltussen et al. 2021; Rosa 2022 (out-of-sample decay; thresholds help)
10

EURO_OPEN_REVERSAL

Sessionnew
Idea
After a US sell-off into the close, dealers carry inventory. Prices rebound strongly when Europe opens; after rallies the effect is much weaker.
Entry
Signal at the bar before the European-open bar (default 01:00 CT) if the previous session's SB closed below its Lower band. Fill at the European-open bar.
Exit
Stop 1 tick below the session's lowest low up to and including the signal bar. Time cap 4 bars. Flat-by.
Basis
Boyarchenko, Larsen & Whelan 2023, RFS (the overnight drift; asymmetric after sell-offs). The paper studies US equities, so CL, GC and SI are tested as an extension.

Families of v2 not carried

The following v2 families were not carried, each for the reason given:

  • cusum_event: fired on 39% of bars.
  • surprise_break: meaningless once z is prior-window.
  • vwap_reversion: a second band on the same idea.
  • shape_rejection: candlestick evidence is negative.
  • ad_divergence_*: AD offset and volume-source problems.
  • range_fade: merged into QUIET_RECOVERY.
  • the early-entry forms: these enter before the reversal is confirmed.

The measurements and sources are in DISC_RESEARCH.md.

T3 ENTRY LAB — BUILD FRAMEWORK

Every candidate in the 5000-row registry, on every instrument, through one fixed sequence of phases. A phase starts only when the previous phase closed with PASS. A phase that closes with STOP ends the program, and that is a valid result.

THE LOCKED PILLARS

1COST REALITY FROM THE FIRST TRADE

Every figure in every phase is net of costs: the round-trip cost in ticks on every trade, plus extra slippage ticks on every stop fill. No gross figure is ever computed, printed or used to rank.

2NEXT-OPEN EXECUTION, CONSERVATIVE FILLS, FLAT BY SESSION END

A signal is known at the close of bar t and is filled at the open of bar t+1. When the stop and the target are both inside one bar, the stop wins. A gap through a level fills at the open. No position is held across a session end or a contract roll.

3INSTRUMENTS ARE INDEPENDENT

The unit of research is candidate × instrument: 5000 × 5 = 25,000 units. Evidence is never pooled across instruments. A candidate that works on CL and fails on NQ is a CL unit, nothing more.

4EVERY TRIAL IS COUNTED, EVERY WINDOW IS LOOKED AT ONCE

All 25,000 screened units and every parameter set tried are counted, and that count feeds the deflation statistics. The Validation window is opened once (P5), the Test window is opened once (P6). Nothing seen in them is fed back into any choice.

5ATR AND TICKS, NEVER PERCENT

All results are in ticks and in RATR (net profit divided by the ATR of the signal bar). The data is back-adjusted additively and can be negative, so percentage returns are never used.

PART A — THE ARCHITECTURE

A.1 — The governing principle

A unit earns trust only by surviving every gate, in order. One failed hard gate removes the unit from every later phase. The program narrows: 25,000 units → shortlist (≤ 50) → refined → evidence survivors → validation survivors → test survivors → live candidates. Parameters are chosen only where the framework says they may be chosen (P3, Dev training blocks); everywhere else they are frozen.

A.2 — The gate law

  1. Every gate is a fixed rule with a fixed threshold written in config.yaml before P2 runs.
  2. Hard gates decide. Report gates are printed beside the result and decide nothing.
  3. Each phase writes gates.json with every gate's value, rule and result, and closes as PASS (all hard phase gates passed) or STOP.
  4. The next phase reads gates.json of the previous phase and refuses to start on STOP.
  5. Unit-level gates are columns in the phase table (g_*). A unit moves forward only if every one of its hard gate columns is true.

A.3 — The three windows

Each instrument's history is split by session, in time order. The split is made once in P0 and never moved.

PART B — STANDING LAWS

B.1 — Never changes

  1. The raw files are never modified. Repairs (duplicates, OHLC containment) are made on the loaded copy and counted in P0.
  2. config.yaml is frozen once P2 has run. Any change restarts the program from P2 in a new output folder (B.20).
  3. Validation numbers never change a parameter, a gate or a candidate list. Test numbers never change anything.
  4. No result is ranked on gross P&L, win rate or profit factor. The ranking metric is always net RATR on the per-session series.
  5. No filter, session restriction or date restriction is added after seeing a losing period. A different rule is a different candidate and must go through P2.
  6. The in-sample equity curve (final parameters on all Dev) is never evidence. Evidence is walk-forward out-of-sample (P4), Validation (P5) and Test (P6).
  7. A single best cell of a parameter grid is never chosen. Choices are plateau-smoothed (B.12) and neighbours must work (B.16).

B.2 — The execution law

  1. Signal: computed from bars ≤ t only, evaluated at the close of bar t.
  2. Entry: market order filled at the open of bar e = t + 1 (t + 2 in the delay stress test).
  3. No entry if bar e belongs to another session or another contract than bar t, if e is beyond the window, or if ATR[t] is not positive.
  4. One position at a time (pyramiding = 0): signals while a trade is open are ignored; a signal on the exit bar is allowed.
  5. Shorts are run on mirrored prices (o, h, l, c) → (−o, −l, −h, −c), so every long rule has an exact short twin.
  6. Sessions: a CME session starts at 17:00 CT (session date = timestamp + 7 h); any gap ≥ 120 minutes also starts a new session. A roll is any change of the contract column.

B.3 — The barrier law (SL, TP, time, session end)

ATR      = Wilder ATR(14) of the signal bar t
SL ticks = max(1, round(SL_ATR × ATR / tick))            stop   = entry − SL ticks × tick
TP ticks = max(1, round(TP_R  × SL ticks))               target = entry + TP ticks × tick
last bar = min(e + max_bars − 1, last bar of the session, last bar before a roll)
  1. Stop and target are fixed at the fill and never move.
  2. For each bar j from e to the last bar:
    • j > e and open ≤ stop → exit at the open (SL, gap through). j > e and open ≥ target → exit at the open (TP).
    • low ≤ stop and high ≥ target in the same bar → SL at the stop (pessimistic). With intrabar: tv the TradingView path is used instead: open nearer the high → high first → TP; otherwise SL.
    • only low ≤ stop → SL at the stop; only high ≥ target → TP at the target.
  3. No barrier hit by bar e + max_bars − 1 → TIME exit at the open of bar e + max_bars, if that bar is in the same session and contract.
  4. The session or the contract ends first → EOS exit at the close of its last bar.
  5. SL_ATR = 0 means no stop and no target (pure time exit). This is the screen exit of P2.
net ticks = (exit − entry) / tick − cost_ticks_rt − (stop_slip_ticks if SL)
R_ATR     = net ticks × tick / ATR
MFE_ATR   = max over held bars of (min(high, target) − entry) / ATR
MAE_ATR   = max over held bars of (entry − max(low, stop)) / ATR

B.9 — The registry law

  1. A candidate is one registry row: trigger, filter, side, T3 length, T3 volume factor.
  2. Its executable identity is spec_hash = sha1 of (trigger, filter, side, T3 length, T3 vf, definitions version). Its idea identity is cell_id = trigger | filter | side.
  3. Every row carries trigger_def and filter_def, the exact formulas of B.7 and B.8, and registry_flags for overlapping, redundant or contradictory combinations.
  4. The registry is frozen in P1. A new idea is a new row and a new run from P2.

B.11 — The ledger and the trial count

  1. P2 records all 25,000 units in P2/units.csv, passed or not.
  2. P3 records, per unit, the number of configs evaluated (n_configs) and keeps their Dev per-session series for the PBO.
  3. The DSR of a unit in P4 uses N = Keff of its instrument + its n_configs, and V = variance of SR across its instrument's 5000 P2 units.

B.12 — The search law (T3, SL, TP, hold)

  1. Objective: score(config, training blocks) = 25th percentile, over the training blocks, of the block's mean net RATR per session. One training block → that block's mean. Fewer than 30 trades in the training blocks → −∞.
  2. Stage A — T3 (only for rows that use T3): grid L × v = 12 × 6 = 72, each run with the screen exit (8-bar time exit).
  3. Stage B — exits on the Stage-A T3 setting: SL_ATR {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5, 2.0} × TP_R {0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5, 3.0} × max bars {4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32} = 252.
  4. Plateau rule: each grid's scores are replaced by the mean of the finite scores in their 3 × 3 (Stage A) or 3 × 3 × 3 (Stage B) neighbourhood; the highest smoothed score wins. A lone spike cannot win.
  5. Optuna (search: optuna): TPE sampler, 200 trials over the joint space (T3 setting, SL, TP, hold), same objective, no plateau rule; every trial is counted in N.
  6. No machine-learning model is fitted anywhere in this program.

B.13 — The matched-null law (timing versus drift)

The null keeps everything except timing: the same signal series, rolled in time by a random whole number of sessions (≥ 5 from each end) inside the same window, run with the same parameters on the same prices. It keeps the number of signals, their clustering and their time of day. A unit passes only if its real mean beats the null distribution: p = (1 + #{null ≥ observed}) / (B + 1) ≤ 0.05.

B.14 — The Sharpe convention

SR = mean / standard deviation of the per-session series (zeros on no-trade sessions), per session, not annualised. The same definition is used in P2 (V and the universe), P4 (DSR), P6 (DSR) and in every table.

P0 — DATA, SESSIONS & WINDOWS

Job

Load and certify the five 30-minute files, build sessions and rolls, and cut the three windows. Nothing is tested in P0.

Inputs

data/: one CSV per instrument with timestamp, open, high, low, close, volume, contract.

Steps

  1. Load each file; parse timestamps as exchange time (CT); sort by time.
  2. Duplicates: remove repeated timestamps, keeping the last; count them.
  3. OHLC containment: count bars where high < max(open, close) or low > min(open, close); set high = max(o, h, l, c) and low = min(o, h, l, c).
  4. Grid checks: share of timestamps on the 30-minute grid; share of closes on the instrument's tick grid.
  5. Sessions: session date = (timestamp + 7 h) date; a new session also starts after any gap ≥ 120 minutes. Count bars per session; count short sessions (< half the median).
  6. Rolls: flag every change of the contract column. Compare the median |open − previous close| / ATR at roll opens with normal session opens (the back-adjustment must make them similar).
  7. Prices: record the minimum price; non-positive prices confirm that only tick and ATR units may be used (Pillar 5).
  8. Windows: by session — Dev = first 60%, 1 embargo session, Validation = next 20%, 1 embargo session, Test = the rest. Cut Dev into 6 equal blocks B0…B5.
  9. Seal: hash the Test rows (SHA-256 of time and OHLC) and store the hash in gates.json. From now until P6, the Test rows are dropped on load.
  10. Cache each instrument with its session, roll, window, block and session-bucket columns.
Gates (hard)
  1. OHLC repairs ≤ 0.1% of bars (worst instrument).
  2. Timestamps on the 30-minute grid ≥ 99%.
  3. Closes on the tick grid ≥ 99% (otherwise the tick in config.yaml is wrong).
  4. Every window of every instrument has ≥ 250 sessions.
Outputs — data_qa.csv, repairs.csv, splits.csv (start, end, sessions and bars of each window); split_timeline.png (price with Validation and Test shaded); sessions.png (bars per session; bar-hour histogram to confirm the timezone).
Closeout — Forwards the cached, split data. Command: python run.py P0

P1 — REGISTRY & ENGINE ACCEPTANCE

Job

Prove that the engine obeys B.2–B.3 exactly, that every trigger and filter is causal and mirrored, and make every registry row executable. No return is looked at.

Steps

  1. Engine self-tests on synthetic bars:
    1. a target hit fills at the target;
    2. stop and target in one bar → stop (pessimistic);
    3. the same bar with the open nearer the high → target under the TradingView path;
    4. a gap through the stop fills at the open;
    5. the time exit fills at the open of bar entry + max_bars;
    6. a session end closes at the last close, and no entry comes from a session's last bar;
    7. signals during a trade are ignored;
    8. the round-trip cost is deducted.
  2. Signal self-tests on a synthetic market:
    1. every short rule equals the long rule on mirrored prices;
    2. changing bars after t never changes any trigger or filter at bars ≤ t (no look-ahead);
    3. T3 coefficients sum to 1 and T3 of a constant is the constant;
    4. every trigger and filter fires at least once;
    5. no simulated trade crosses a session end or a roll.
  3. Canonicalise the registry (B.9): for each of the 5000 rows write trigger_def, filter_def, cell_id, spec_hash, uses_t3 (recomputed from the trigger and filter names) and registry_flags:
    • overlaps_DONCHIAN3_PREVBAR: DONCHIAN_3 and PREVBAR_BREAK fire almost together;
    • redundant_pair: the filter repeats the trigger (GAP + GAP_ALIGNED, RANGE_EXPANSION + RANGE_EXPAND or CLV);
    • contradictory_pair: RANGE_EXPANSION_CLOSE + RANGE_COMPRESS_060;
    • no_t3_neighbour: a T3 cell present with a single T3 setting;
    • uses_t3_mismatch: the registry's uses_t3 disagrees with the rule.
  4. Registry checks: unknown triggers or filters; duplicate spec hashes; side values.
  5. Trigger overlap: on Dev bars of every instrument, long side, T3 3 / 0.9, the Jaccard overlap of the 32 trigger signal sets; list pairs ≥ 0.5.
Gates (hard)
  1. All self-tests pass (14 / 14).
  2. 0 registry rows with an unknown trigger or filter.
  3. Unique spec hashes = number of rows.
  4. Every side is long or short.
Outputs — selftests.csv, candidate_registry_fixed.csv, registry_flags.csv, near_duplicate_triggers.csv; registry_composition.png (rows per trigger × filter), trigger_overlap.png (Jaccard matrix).
Closeout — Forwards the frozen canonical registry. Command: python run.py P1

P2 — SCREEN (ALL 5000 CANDIDATES × 5 INSTRUMENTS)

Job

Rank entries, not exits. Every unit is run on Dev with one identical screen exit, net of costs, and must show an edge that survives the size of the search.

Steps

  1. Signals: for each instrument and each registry row, signal = trigger ∧ filter (B.7, B.8) with the row's own T3 setting.
  2. Screen exit: no stop, no target, TIME exit at the open of bar entry + 8 (EOS at the session end if sooner), full costs (B.3, B.4).
  3. Per-unit metrics on Dev:
    • trades n, mean net RATR per trade, win rate, profit factor;
    • median MFE and median MAE (ATR);
    • the per-session series, its mean, Sharpe and Newey-West t, with one-sided p = 1 − Φ(t);
    • the number of Dev blocks (of 6) with a positive sum.
  4. Universe statistics per instrument:
    • Keff (A.4);
    • V = variance of SR across its 5000 units;
    • Hansen SPA p (500 stationary-bootstrap draws);
    • PBO of choosing the Dev winner (CSCV, 16 blocks);
    • the best SR.
  5. Multiple testing: Benjamini-Hochberg over all 25,000 p-values at q = 0.10. Print the luck line: units with t ≥ 2 expected by chance = 25,000 × (1 − Φ(2)) ≈ 570.
  6. Unit gates (below) → the passing set.
  7. De-duplication within each instrument: sort the passing units by t; keep a unit only if the Jaccard overlap of its entry bars with every kept unit is ≤ 0.30.
  8. Shortlist: the top 50 remaining units by t.
  9. Horizon profile of the shortlist: mean net R for time exits of 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16 bars.
Gates — per unit (hard, all required)
  1. g_trades: n ≥ 100.
  2. g_mean: mean net R per trade > 0.
  3. g_t: Newey-West t ≥ 2.0.
  4. g_bh: BH q ≤ 0.10 over 25,000.
  5. g_blocks: ≥ 4 of 6 Dev blocks positive.
  6. g_mfe_mae: median MFE > median MAE.
Gate — phase: shortlist ≥ 1 unit.
Outputs — units.csv (all 25,000), universe.csv, gate_funnel.csv, shortlist.csv; screen_distribution.png (t histogram of each instrument against N(0,1); volcano mean vs −log10 p), family_heatmap.png (share of t ≥ 2 by family × instrument), mfe_mae_horizons.png (MFE vs MAE; horizon profile).
Closeout — Forwards the shortlist (≤ 50 units) and the universe statistics (Keff, V, SPA p) to P3 and P4. Command: python run.py P2

P3 — T3 SETTINGS, SL / TP / HOLD (WALK-FORWARD IN DEV)

Job

For each shortlisted unit, choose the T3 setting and the exit (stop in ATR, target in R, maximum bars held) without ever scoring a choice on the data it was chosen on. The result is one walk-forward out-of-sample series per unit and one final parameter set.

Steps

  1. Folds: Dev blocks B0…B5. Fold k = 1…5 trains on B0…B(k−1) and is scored on Bk.
  2. Objective (B.12): 25th percentile of the training blocks' mean net R per session; −∞ below 30 training trades.
  3. Stage A — T3 setting (T3 units only): score the 72 settings L × v with the screen exit on the fold's training blocks; plateau-smooth the 12 × 6 grid; take the best. Non-T3 units keep their single setting.
  4. Stage B — stop, target, hold on the Stage-A setting: score the 252 exits on the same training blocks. Each exit means:
    • stop = SL_ATR × ATR below the fill;
    • target = TP_R × the stop distance above it;
    • a time exit after max bars;
    • the session-end exit always applies (B.3).
    Plateau-smooth the 6 × 7 × 6 grid; take the best.
  5. Score the fold: run the chosen parameters, untouched, on block Bk; keep its per-session results and trade count.
  6. Stitch blocks B1…B5 into the walk-forward out-of-sample (WF-OOS) series.
  7. Final parameters: repeat Stages A and B on all six blocks. These are the parameters frozen for P4–P7.
  8. Ledger and PBO: count every evaluated config (n_configs); compute the PBO (CSCV, 16 blocks) over the Dev per-session series of all evaluated configs.
  9. Optuna alternative (search: optuna): steps 3–4 are replaced by 200 TPE trials over the joint space in every fold and for the final set; all trials are counted.
Gates — per unit (hard)
  1. WF-OOS trades ≥ 50.
  2. WF-OOS mean per session > 0.
Gate — phase: ≥ 1 unit passes.
Outputs — refined.csv (final T3 length, vf, SL_ATR, TP_R, max bars, n_configs, PBO, WF-OOS mean, trades, positive folds), wf_folds.csv (parameters chosen and OOS result of every fold), units/*.npz (WF-OOS series, fold sums); exit_surfaces.png (Dev score over SL × TP at the chosen hold, star = choice), wf_folds.png (OOS result per fold).
Closeout — Forwards the passing units with their final parameters, WF-OOS series, fold sums, n_configs and PBO. Command: python run.py P3

P4 — SELECTION BIAS, NULL, ROBUSTNESS & STABILITY

Job

Decide whether each refined unit is real. Evidence comes from its walk-forward out-of-sample series, corrected for the full search, tested against random timing, stressed, and checked across time, volatility and sessions.

Steps

  1. Bootstrap the WF-OOS series (2,000 stationary-bootstrap draws): 95% CI and one-sided p of the mean.
  2. Deflated Sharpe of the WF-OOS series with N = Keff(instrument) + n_configs and V from P2.
  3. PBO of the unit's P3 search; SPA p of its instrument's P2 universe.
  4. Fold dispersion: share of the 5 folds with a positive sum; largest fold sum ÷ sum of the positive folds.
  5. Final parameters on Dev: run them once; these trades feed steps 6–10.
  6. Circular-shift null (B.13), 1,000 draws, on Dev.
  7. Stress (B.16): cost × 1.5; +1 tick per side; +1 bar entry delay.
  8. Neighbours (B.16): every axis one grid step down and up; median of the Dev means.
  9. Concentration: mean trade without the best 5% of trades; total without the best calendar year.
  10. Stability (B.15): years, volatility regimes, session buckets.
Gates — per unit (hard, all required)
  1. g_ci_lo: 95% bootstrap CI lower bound of the WF-OOS mean > 0.
  2. g_dsr: DSR ≥ 0.95.
  3. g_null: circular-shift null p ≤ 0.05.
  4. g_pbo: PBO of the P3 search ≤ 0.40.
  5. g_spa: SPA p of the instrument's universe ≤ 0.10.
  6. g_wf_pos: ≥ 70% of folds positive (≥ 4 of 5).
  7. g_wf_share: no fold above 50% of the positive-fold total.
  8. g_cost: mean > 0 at cost × 1.5.
  9. g_slip: mean > 0 with +1 tick per side.
  10. g_delay: mean > 0 with a one-bar entry delay.
  11. g_neighbours: neighbours' median > 0.
  12. g_top_trim: mean > 0 without the top 5% of trades.
  13. g_best_year: total > 0 without the best year.
  14. g_years: ≥ 60% of years positive.
  15. g_stable: no judged bucket significantly negative; ≤ 1 negative regime bucket; ≤ 1 negative session bucket.
Gate — phase: ≥ 1 unit passes all 15.
Outputs — evidence.csv (every statistic and gate per unit), gate_funnel.csv, survivors.csv; wf_equity_ci.png (WF-OOS equity curves; CI forest), gate_matrix.png (units × gates, green = pass), null_tests.png (null distribution vs observed).
Closeout — Forwards the units that passed all 15 gates, with frozen final parameters. Command: python run.py P4

P5 — VALIDATION (ONE LOOK, PARAMETERS FROZEN)

Job

Run every P4 survivor once on the Validation window with its frozen parameters. Nothing is chosen here.

Steps

  1. Load Dev + Validation (Test rows are still dropped). Build each unit's signal with its frozen T3 setting.
  2. Simulate on the Validation window with its frozen SL_ATR, TP_R and max bars; build the per-session series.
  3. Stationary bootstrap (10,000 draws): mean, 95% CI, one-sided p.
  4. Benjamini-Hochberg across all P5 units at q = 0.05.
  5. Circular-shift null on the Validation window (1,000 draws).
  6. Cost × 1.5 on the Validation window.
Gates — per unit (hard, all required)
  1. g_trades: ≥ 30 Validation trades.
  2. g_mean: Validation mean per session > 0.
  3. g_bh: BH q ≤ 0.05.
  4. g_null: null p ≤ 0.05.
  5. g_cost: mean > 0 at cost × 1.5.
Gate — phase: ≥ 1 unit passes.
Outputs — validation.csv, survivors.csv; validation.png (equity with frozen parameters through Dev and then Validation, Validation shaded; Validation CI forest).
Closeout — Forwards the validation survivors with unchanged parameters. Command: python run.py P5

P6 — SEALED TEST (ONE LOOK) & DEPLOYMENT CARDS

Job

Open the sealed Test window once for the validation survivors, with frozen parameters, and issue a deployment card for every unit that passes.

Steps

  1. Check the LOCK (B.20); write the hash of the unit set being opened.
  2. Load the full history; simulate each unit on the Test window with frozen parameters.
  3. Stationary bootstrap (10,000 draws): mean and 95% CI.
  4. DSR on the Test series with N = number of P6 units and V = variance of their Test SRs (PSR against 0 when N = 1).
  5. Cost × 1.5 on the Test window.
  6. For each passing unit, write the deployment card:
    • instrument, trigger, filter, side;
    • T3 length and vf;
    • ATR length, SL_ATR, TP_R, max bars;
    • round-trip cost.
Gates — per unit (hard, all required)
  1. g_trades: ≥ 30 Test trades.
  2. g_ci_lo: 95% CI lower bound of the Test mean > 0.
  3. g_dsr: DSR ≥ 0.95.
  4. g_cost: mean > 0 at cost × 1.5.
  5. The Test window was not opened before for a different unit set.
Gate — phase: ≥ 1 unit passes.
Outputs — test.csv, deployment_cards.csv, LOCK; test.png (equity over the whole history with Validation and Test shaded; Test CI forest).
Closeout — Forwards the deployment cards to P7. Command: python run.py P6

P7 — PINE PARITY, INCUBATION & KILL SWITCH

Job

Reproduce each deployment card in TradingView, prove that Pine matches the engine, then let real time decide. Performed in TradingView with T3_Entry_Lab_v2.pine.

Steps

  1. Load the card: Trigger, Filter, Side, T3 length, T3 volume factor, ATR length, Stop loss (× ATR), Take profit (R), Max bars in trade; "Close open trade on the last bar of the session" on.
  2. Costs: in Properties, set the commission per contract and the slippage ticks equal to the card's round-trip cost.
  3. Parity replay: set "Trade from / Trade until" to the Test dates in P0/splits.csv; compare the strategy table (trades, win rate, net mean R, MFE / MAE, bars held, exit mix SL / TP / TIME / EOS) with the unit's row in P6/test.csv.
  4. Incubation: run it in paper or simulation from today forward until ≥ 30 trades.
  5. Kill switch (checked after every trade once 30 trades exist): stop the strategy if
    • its live mean RATR falls below the lower bound of its Test 95% CI; or
    • its live drawdown exceeds the worst drawdown of its Dev + Validation + Test history.
  6. Review each quarter: a killed unit is not restarted with new parameters. New parameters are a new program from P2 (B.20).
Gates (hard)
  1. Parity: Pine trade count within ±5% of the engine's Test trade count; Pine net mean R inside the unit's Test 95% CI. (TradingView fills both-barrier bars with its own path; run the engine with intrabar: tv for an exact comparison.)
  2. Incubation: after ≥ 30 trades, live mean RATR ≥ Test CI lower bound and drawdown within the historical worst.
Outputs — parity screenshot of the strategy table per card; live trade log with RATR, MFE / MAE and exit type; kill-switch record.
Closeout — A unit that passes incubation is live. A unit that trips the kill switch is closed for good.
